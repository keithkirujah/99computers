About us

Your trusted partner in IT solutions, electronics, security systems, and technology consultancy.

99 COMPUTERS is dedicated to providing high-quality products and professional services that meet the needs of individuals, homes, businesses, and organizations. Our goal is to make technology accessible, affordable, reliable, and convenient for everyone.

We specialize in IT consultancy, computer sales, electronics, accessories, and CCTV installation services. Our wide range of products includes laptops, desktop computers, televisions, smartphones, washing machines, radios, printers, CCTV cameras, and other electronic devices and accessories.

Our professional services include IT consultancy, computer setup and configuration, technology support, and CCTV camera installation for homes, offices, shops, institutions, and other commercial premises. We help our clients improve security through reliable surveillance solutions designed to monitor their properties, protect their assets, and provide greater peace of mind.

We believe that customer satisfaction begins with quality products, affordable prices, professional service, and lasting relationships. We are committed to keeping up with technological advancements to provide modern solutions that meet the changing needs of our customers.
